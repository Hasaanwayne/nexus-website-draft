1. Who we are

This website, nexuscreativehq.com, is run by NEXUS Creative HQ Ltd, trading as NEXUS (“NEXUS”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

NEXUS Creative HQ Ltd is registered in England and Wales under company number 12667243. Our registered office is Progress House, 404 Brighton Road, South Croydon, CR2 6AN. We are registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) under registration number ZB645413.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Identity and contact details: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Professional details: your organisation, job title, country, sector and the stage of your business.
  • Enquiry details: what you are interested in, your timeline and budget range, and anything you write in a message to us.
  • Event details: your registration and attendance at NEXUS events.
  • Marketing preferences: whether you have agreed to receive our newsletter and updates.
  • Website usage data: pages viewed, time on the site, how you arrived (for example, the link or campaign you followed), and device and browser information. We collect this through cookies and analytics only where you agree.

Our website and services are aimed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

3. How we collect personal data

  • Enquiry, booking and partnership forms on our website.
  • Newsletter and event sign-up forms.
  • Report request forms.
  • Emails, calls and meetings with our team.
  • Event registration and attendance.
  • Cookies and analytics tools, where you agree.

Sometimes we receive your details from someone else, for example a partner institution that introduces your company to us, or a colleague who refers you. We use those details only to respond to the introduction and to provide the services discussed.

4. How we use personal data

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange calls or meetings.
  • To provide our services, including readiness reviews, UK landing support, representation and introductions.
  • To manage event registration, attendance and logistics.
  • To send our newsletter and updates, where you have agreed.
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We publish client case studies, names, logos, quotes and photographs only with the client’s agreement. Events may be photographed or filmed, and that footage may be used to promote and report on our work.

5. Our legal basis for using personal data (UK GDPR)

  • Consent: for our newsletter and marketing emails, and for analytics cookies. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to respond to business enquiries, run our events, develop our services and keep our website secure, where your rights do not override those interests.
  • Contract: to take steps you ask for before entering a contract with us, and to provide services you have agreed to buy.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

6. Marketing

We send our newsletter and updates only to people who have signed up or otherwise agreed. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us. Unsubscribing does not stop messages we need to send about an enquiry, service or event you are involved in.

7. Who we share personal data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run our website and business, and only as far as they need it:

  • specialist partners in our network (for example law, accountancy or finance firms), but only when you ask us to introduce you or agree to it;
  • our professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants;
  • regulators, law enforcement or other authorities where the law requires it.

8. Our endorsing role

NEXUS also acts as an endorsing body for the UK Innovator Founder route under UK Endorsing Services. Endorsement applications are handled through a separate process, which has its own privacy notice. Information you share through this website for our commercial services is not used in endorsement decisions.

9. International transfers

Some of our service providers process personal data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we use safeguards recognised under UK law, such as UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

10. How long we keep personal data

  • Enquiries and form submissions: two years from our last contact with you, unless you become a client.
  • Client records: for as long as we provide services to you, and then as long as the law requires, usually six years for financial records.
  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your details.
  • Event registrations: as long as we need them to run the event and meet legal obligations.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.

We may keep data for longer where we need it to establish, bring or defend legal claims.

11. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies. Analytics cookies are only set if you agree. Please see our Cookie Policy for details and to change your choices.

12. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data;
  • ask for your data in a portable format, where this applies;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects. We will respond to requests within one calendar month.

13. How to contact us

For any question about this policy or your personal data, email web@nexuscreativehq.com or write to: Privacy Enquiries, NEXUS Creative HQ Ltd, Progress House, 404 Brighton Road, South Croydon, CR2 6AN.

14. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be on this page, and we will take reasonable steps to tell you about significant changes.
