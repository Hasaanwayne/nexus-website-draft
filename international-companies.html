Who it is for

You are a technology company with a product in market, funding or revenue, and a decision-maker committed to UK growth.

Not for you if

Your UK questions

Do I need a UK company to start selling?

It depends on your business model and customers. The diagnostic covers this, and our legal and accountancy partners advise on the right structure.

Do you only work with Chinese companies?

No. China is our flagship corridor, and we work with technology companies from other markets that are serious about the UK.

Why is the diagnostic paid?

Because it produces a real plan you can act on. The fee is credited against further work if you continue with us.
