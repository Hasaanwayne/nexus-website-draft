About the company

Eco2Life is building EcoDo, an AI-enabled platform that helps brands expanding into new markets turn strategy and local insight into content and campaigns that actually land.

The challenge

Turning a founder-led consultancy into a scalable platform business, which meant UK innovation ecosystem access, funding readiness and the right research and commercial partners.

How NEXUS helped

NEXUS acted as the operational bridge into the UK innovation ecosystem, opening routes into a leading university partnership engagement, Innovate UK grant preparation and fundraising readiness, and an international market route from Britain into Shanghai and Singapore.

The outcome

Eco2Life has moved from consultancy to a live platform with early revenue, a UK patent application in progress and a cross-disciplinary team, and is now funding and partnership ready.

Impact at a glance

  • Consultancy to a live AI platform with early revenue.
  • UK patent application in progress.
  • Routes opened into university research and innovation grant support.
