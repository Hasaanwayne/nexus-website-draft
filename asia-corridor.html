Our partners in China

Three of our partnerships, with more in development. Each gives UK companies a landing point in China, and gives Chinese companies a trusted route to the UK.

China · Our operating partner

RidgeCore Ventures

Our strategic partner on the ground in China.

RidgeCore Ventures is our strategic partner in China, based in Hainan. It represents NEXUS in the Chinese market and runs our programmes on the ground, from meetings and introductions to interpreters and logistics. Two locally incorporated teams with one purpose: we don’t make an introduction and step back. We become your team in the market, in both directions.

Tianjin · Government partner

Tianjin Binhai High-Tech Area

A landing base in one of China’s national high-tech zones.

Our partnership with Tianjin Binhai High-Tech Area, the city’s national-level high-tech zone, gives UK companies a base in the Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei region, with introductions to local companies and investors through the zone’s venture capital community. Together we run roadshows and matchmaking events, and bring Tianjin technology companies to the UK.

Shanghai · Europe and China

Swiss Bluefaer Innovation Centre

A landing address in Shanghai, linking Europe and China.

A Swiss innovation centre in Shanghai’s Yangpu district, linking Switzerland and China. Under a three-year strategic partnership signed in Shanghai in May 2026, NEXUS and RidgeCore are Bluefaer’s UK partners, supporting its companies as they expand into the UK. In return, Bluefaer gives our UK cohorts a landing address in Yangpu.

Common questions

Insights on doing business in China→
Can NEXUS represent my company in China?

Yes, through our reciprocal partnership with RidgeCore Ventures. We handle UK representation for their clients, and they handle China representation for ours. Together we open access to investment, knowledge transfer and franchise opportunities.

Why is Hong Kong part of the corridor?

Hong Kong is a familiar, international base for UK companies taking their first step into Asia, and a launch point for Chinese companies heading to the UK and Europe.

Am I committing to anything when I register interest in a 2027 delegation?

No. Registering interest carries no commitment. We share the programme and costs when they are confirmed. Places are limited for each trip.

Where will the 2027 delegations go, and what will they cost?

Shanghai and Tianjin, plus an inbound programme for Chinese companies at London Tech Week. The dates and focus of each trip are in the 2027 delegations table above. We share the programme and costs when each trip is confirmed.

Do you also help companies from China enter the UK?

Yes. We work with technology companies entering the UK, and with the science parks, innovation zones and funds that back them. See UK market entry for international companies and UK partnerships for institutions. UK companies already trading can start with a growth diagnostic for UK scale-ups.

Helping technology businesses land, grow and scale in the UK, with China as our flagship corridor.
