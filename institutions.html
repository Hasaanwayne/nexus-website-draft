Showing results abroad

“We need international outcomes for our companies, but a UK team takes years to build.”

Innovation institutions are expected to show results abroad: companies landing, partnerships signed, investment won. Doing that from a distance is slow, and opening an overseas office is expensive and hard to justify.

NEXUS gives you an operational presence in the UK from day one, run by a team that already works on both sides of the UK–China corridor.

Who it is for

UK representation is built for institutions whose companies are ready to grow internationally, with a programme behind every introduction.

This is for you if

You lead a government-linked innovation zone, science park, incubator or corporate venture fund, and your companies are ready to grow internationally.

This is not for you if

You are looking for a one-off introduction or a speaking slot without a programme behind it.

How it works

How a UK representation partnership runs, from the first call to the yearly review.

  1. Partnership call

    We learn about your goals, your companies and your timelines.

  2. Proposal

    A scoped plan with deliverables, reporting and price.

  3. Agreement

    We agree terms and onboard your team and first companies.

  4. Delivery

    Events, introductions and landing support, with regular reports.

  5. Review

    We review outcomes together and plan the following year.
