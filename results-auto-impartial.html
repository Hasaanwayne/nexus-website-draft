About the company

Auto Impartial is building a next-generation AI compute architecture that lets regulated enterprises run powerful AI inference inside their own walls, without depending on scarce, high-cost data centre hardware.

The challenge

A world-class chip architect with a validated design but based outside the UK and without the local networks, funding routes or international research partners needed to take the work from proven concept towards silicon.

How NEXUS helped

NEXUS supported Dr Huang’s move to the UK and helped establish the company here, then positioned Auto Impartial inside the UK frontier technology ecosystem. NEXUS is now helping form the international consortium and shape the Innovate UK collaborative research and development bid that funds the next stage.

The outcome

Auto Impartial is established in the UK with its core architecture independently validated and is building a UK and Asia research consortium and a costed route to silicon, targeting a global market for private AI inference that is growing several fold this decade.

Impact at a glance

  • Core compute architecture independently validated.
  • UK and Asia research consortium in formation with Innovate UK.
  • Targeting private, on-premises AI inference for regulated sectors.
