1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. They help the site work, remember your choices, and, if you agree, help us understand how visitors use the site.

2. How we use cookies

We use two kinds of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies keep the website secure and remember your cookie choices. The site cannot work properly without them, so they do not need your consent.
  • Analytics cookies tell us which pages people visit and how they found us, so we can improve the site. We only set these if you click “Accept” on our cookie banner.

We do not use advertising cookies.

4. Managing your choices

  • On our website: you can accept or reject analytics cookies when you first visit. You can change your mind at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page.
  • In your browser: most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, parts of the site may not work.
  • Google Analytics: you can also install Google’s opt-out tool at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Rejecting analytics cookies does not affect how the website works for you.

5. More information

For how we handle personal data more generally, see our Privacy Policy. If you have a question about cookies, email web@nexuscreativehq.com.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when we change the cookies we use. The current version will always be on this page.
